What happens next

  1. Chaincode draws an inspector from the tx hash. You can't choose. Conflicted inspectors are excluded.
  2. Inspector weighs, grades and checks the seal independently.
  3. Within tolerance: receipt minted with the inspector's values.
  4. Outside tolerance: no receipt, MINT_MISMATCH dispute opens for the regulator.
